## Credit terms

The workspace is intended for adults aged 18 or over who can legally accept these terms. Free workspace enrollment gives this signed-in account 3 credits once, not monthly. Paid plans, payments, automatic billing, paid conversion and credit top-ups are coming soon. Enrollment currently requires no card and creates no charges. Future paid features will require separate terms and explicit authorization before any charge. One credit saves a new workflow and includes downloads of that workflow revision. Changes saved before its first download share that credit; saving a changed workflow after download uses one new credit. Repeated downloads of the same saved revision do not use credits. AI suggestions, rejected saves and failed transactions do not use credits. Testing and releasing remain subject to service safety limits and successful verification; a credit does not guarantee an agent result or availability. Credits have no cash value, are not transferable and currently have no scheduled expiry. Closing enrollment stops new saves and downloads, without erasing retained workflows or restoring previously used credits. Rejoining does not repeat the welcome grant. You may request correction of an erroneous deduction at contact@agentconcierge.biz. Material changes require a new notice and cannot authorize future payments. Your mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

## Data use notice

Account identifiers, sign-in sessions, saved workflows, agreement records and credit history are used to provide and secure the workspace. AI generation shares only your separately authorized public objective and approved capability references with the suggestion service. Do not submit personal, confidential or sensitive information. Each agent test has its own input-sharing review. Enrollment does not authorize advertising or model training. You can close enrollment here or delete your account in Your organizations. Workspace credit and agreement records are kept while the account exists, then removed within 90 days after account deletion. Other security records follow the service Privacy notice; contact contact@agentconcierge.biz for access, correction, deletion or questions. Read the service Privacy notice for storage, retention and your rights.

## Implementation

Customer-only credit accounts are distinct from provider settlement and TEST_UNIT metering. Enrollment is explicit and versioned. Necessary service processing uses the applicable contractual basis; the privacy checkbox is an acknowledgment, not a blanket consent. Advertising remains separate. A signed ledger, bounded integer balances, exact replay keys and one SQLite transaction bind saves and debits. Factory reviews bind the quoted credit amount, so downloading between review and commit requires a fresh review. Pre-download derived drafts share an entitlement; a saved post-download revision creates a new entitlement. Existing unmetered drafts require a newly saved metered revision to download; no historical debit is inferred. Distinct Google/GitHub accounts are not merged by email. Account deletion removes feature records after 90 days through bounded startup/read cleanup; service activity is required for cleanup to run. Paid plans, top-up transactions, automatic billing and paid conversion are Coming soon. Current enrollment creates no charge. Calendar refills are not part of the initial grant. Provider settlement remains separate.

Staff can inspect 100 accounts per search, signed agreement records and the latest 100 events. Only a current superadmin can adjust credits or suspend/reinstate an existing enrolled account, with revision, explicit reason and idempotent command. Staff cannot fabricate initial customer assent. Closing enrollment is available without contacting staff; re-enrollment does not repeat the grant.
