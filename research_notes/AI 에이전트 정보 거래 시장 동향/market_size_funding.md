# Agent-to-Agent Data/Service Trading Economy: Market Size, Real Demand, and Investment Flows (as of Oct 12, 2026)

## Q1. Latest measured volumes (x402, Virtuals ACP, Olas, others) and which service categories get paid most

### Takeaway
x402 is the best-measured rail. It has a large cumulative transaction count (~192M) but small dollar volume (~$42M cumulative; recently about $25k–$45k per day in raw settlement). Dollar volume peaked in Q4 2025 and is down about 93–96% from there. Virtuals' "aGDP" (~$480M) is a self-reported gross-turnover metric that includes trading value; actual agent revenue there is around $1M. Olas A2A turnover is under $100k. Paid services cluster in crypto/DeFi/trading data, search/data APIs, and AI inference, but revenue is highly concentrated and partly self-traded.

### Inferences
- Cumulative x402 volume is about $42M over roughly one year. Annualizing current raw run-rates of about $30k–$45k/day gives about $11–16M/yr gross settlement, and the organic portion is plausibly only single-digit $M/yr.
- Transaction counts (tens of millions per month) are a misleading headline because the average ticket is $0.04–$0.22.
- Virtuals aGDP is not comparable to x402 settlement volume: it counts trading notional. Real ACP service revenue is on the order of $1M cumulative (March 2026).
- The best real-demand signal is agents paying APIs for crypto data, search and inference, plus a few consumer-utility merchants such as Bitrefill. Revenue is extremely concentrated.

## Q2. Analyses of fake/inflated volume (wash, test, leaderboard farming)

### Takeaway
Every independent analysis finds a large non-organic share of activity. Artemis/Visa removed about 39% of x402 transactions and about 89% of dollar volume as wash/test/internal. Coinbase itself estimates 25–30% of transactions came from leaderboard farming. Chainalysis attributes the Q4 2025 surge to meme-coin farming.

### Cited Findings
- Artemis/Visa report "Agentic Payments from the Ground Up" (published July 2026, data as of Apr 21, 2026):
  - Raw: about 178.3M txs and about $135.7M volume. Adjusted: about 109.6M txs and about $15.0M volume. That removes about 39% of txs and about 89% of USD as wash, test or internal transfers.
  - About 5,300 sellers over the protocol's life, and an average adjusted payment of about $0.14.
  - Methodology: a seller is a wallet that received 3 or more adjusted txs from 2 or more distinct buyers. The wash filter thresholds and code are not published.
  - Note: the raw $135.7M conflicts with the ~$42M cumulative in Dune-based trackers, which reflects definitional and scope differences. — [McGlynn, Sep 6, 2026](https://www.danielmcglynn.com/the-x402-counter-has-shown-the-same-four-numbers-since-march/)
- Artemis, March 2026: about $28k daily volume, and roughly half of transactions looked like self-trading or wash. An Artemis analyst's self-dealing/cycling filter gave a 30-day adjusted volume of $1.6M. — [Bitget news](https://www.bitget.com/news/detail/12560605255618); [Artemis on X](https://x.com/artemis/status/2031768320081277029)
- Coinbase (Lincoln Murr, head of AI product, Aug 23, 2026): 25–30% of x402 transactions "may have been generated by users trying to climb public leaderboards rather than buying services they needed." He called the current stage the "Napster/LimeWire era". — [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/08/23/crypto-s-next-billion-users-might-be-ai-agents-and-they-re-paying-with-stablecoins)
- Chainalysis (Jun 3, 2026): "Much of the growth was driven by meme coin farming activity" (PING token mint via x402, Oct 2025). Retention dropped from 87% to 5%. x402 payer wallets are much younger (197 vs 423 days average age) and hold more tokens (26 vs 4) than typical Base wallets, a speculative-user profile. — [Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/x402-agentic-payments-adoption/)
- Decipher Club (Sep 23, 2026): argues most top x402 sellers are self-trades, and organic 30-day merchant revenue is about $76k. — [Decipher Club](https://www.decipherclub.com/why-x402-merchants-are-poor/)
- The public x402.org counter is stale and unfiltered, unchanged from at least July through Sep 6, 2026. — [McGlynn](https://www.danielmcglynn.com/the-x402-counter-has-shown-the-same-four-numbers-since-march/)
- Virtuals aGDP includes trading turnover and is not independently reproduced. — [BEX, May 9, 2026](https://bex.co/blog/2026/05/09/virtuals-protocol-ai-economic-operating-system-on-chain-agent-economy)

### Inferences
- A reasonable central estimate: 30–50% of x402 transaction count and 80–90%+ of historical USD volume is non-organic.
- Since the Q4 2025 speculative wave ended, the remaining (smaller) activity is likely a higher-quality mix. One analysis suggested the wash share declined over H1 2026, but this was not verified.
- "Autonomous agent" activity cannot be cleanly separated from human-scripted bots on-chain. No source claims a reliable classifier.

### Gaps
- No TRM Labs or OKX Ventures x402/agent-payment wash analysis was found in this session.
- No published, reproducible wash-filter code from Artemis.
- No estimate of the wash share for Virtuals ACP or Olas.

## Q4. Analyst forecasts for agentic commerce (McKinsey, Gartner, Morgan Stanley, Bain, etc.) and their credibility

### Takeaway
Forecasts span roughly 1,000x, from about $5B (niche software market) to $3–5T (McKinsey, by 2030). The spread is driven by definition: AI-influenced GMV versus agent-completed checkout versus vendor revenue. None of them measures the agent-to-agent data/API economy, which today is in the tens of millions of dollars.

- Credibility: they are scenario-based, published Oct–Dec 2025 at peak hype, with no measured baseline. McKinsey's upper bound depends on a broad "influenced" definition. Morgan Stanley and Bain are more conservative and checkout-based.

## Q5. Unit economics: price per call, take rates, top-seller revenue

### Takeaway
x402 calls are micropayments: the measured average is $0.04–$0.30 depending on source and window, and the adjusted average is about $0.14. Facilitators largely do not charge a protocol take rate, compared with 2–4% card acceptance. Organic per-merchant revenue is tiny: about $76k across 2,252 merchants in a 30-day window, with a single merchant (Bitrefill) at about $29k.

### Inferences
- At $0.01–$0.30 per call and no protocol take rate, value capture today sits with the stablecoin issuer (USDC float), the L2/chain, facilitators' strategic value (Coinbase CDP, Stripe) and token incentives, not with transaction fees.
- At ~$34/merchant/month on average, most x402 sellers are hobby or experimental. Real businesses use x402 as an additional channel (Cloudflare, Bitrefill) rather than a primary revenue source.
