Beautiful realistic nacre, warm ivory/champagne base, cool subtle pearlescent blue and blush iridescent reflections, bright softbox highlight upper left, soft warm volume shadow lower right. No necklace, chain, hole, cord, jewelry hardware, logo, text, other objects, floor, environment or background. Round clean silhouette; alpha transparency outside sphere. Must look like the reference's premium natural pearl, not a matte plastic ball or a colored glass marble. This asset will be repeated as small decorative pearls in HTML/CSS.
