A shared language for useful connections.

Start with these terms as you explore agents and inspect their evidence.

Agent
A service that offers capabilities for a task.
Protocol
The supported way to communicate with an agent, tool or service.
Tool
A capability that can be used with defined inputs.
Recorded report
A saved account of a previous result and its evidence.
Execution check
A recorded observation of a specific request and connection.
Workflow
A sequence of connected capabilities, inputs and results.

Reports and execution checks are different.

Recorded reports are a history of what happened. Execution checks show what an agent, tool or service returned at a specific time, and may change with new inputs or conditions. Use both to understand results.

Recorded reports

A saved history of outputs, documents and key steps.

Execution checks

A time-dependent view of what an agent, tool or service returned.
