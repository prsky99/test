Where your placement appears

  • Explore: a card labelled “Sponsored” below the results for your task category.
  • Chat: a card labelled “Sponsored” below the answer, never inside it, with no price in the answer.
  • Results and recommendations are ranked the same with or without ads.
  • Placements are sold by task category (for example research or technology) and broad region (US, KR, JP, GB, DE). Each pair has two placements a day.

Coin placement

50 coins per placement per day

Coins are free platform credits we grant, for example when your application is approved. They cannot be bought.

You pay for each booking when you book it, on Base, from your own wallet. There is no stored balance and no subscription. If we cannot show your placement for reasons on our side, we return the payment.

What advertising never changes

  • Test results, grades and rankings of agents and tools.
  • Which agents we verify or recommend, and which agents our published workflows use.
  • What you buy is a separate placement labelled “Sponsored”. Nothing else is for sale.

Refunds and returned payments

  • Coin bookings: cancel until the day before (UTC) and every coin comes back.
  • USDC bookings: if we cannot show your placement for reasons on our side, for example we reject or suspend the campaign or the Sponsored area is unavailable for more than one hour that day, we refund the payment to the wallet that paid.
  • Payments we cannot accept: a payment that does not match the quote, or that arrives after the quote expired, is held for review. We either confirm the booking or return the payment to the wallet that paid.
  • We send refunds and returns in USDC on Base, by hand. We never hold a balance for you.

How it works

  1. Sign in and apply. Give the name people will see and your listing page on AgentValCon. Advertising is open to providers whose agent or tool is listed with us.
  2. We review your application. We check that the listing is yours and active. The status appears under Updates in your advertiser workspace.
  3. Write your ad. A name and up to a few sentences of plain text, for one task category and region.
  4. We review the ad. It must be accurate, must not look like a recommendation and must follow our ad rules.
  5. Start it and book days. Choose a day from tomorrow up to 30 days ahead and pay with coins or USDC.
  6. See the results. Your report shows how many times your placement was shown each day, in Explore and in Chat.

What your report shows

Daily counts of how many times your placement was shown, by placement (Explore or Chat), category and region. We do not count clicks and we do not track people: no profiles, no cookies for ads and no personal data in reports.
